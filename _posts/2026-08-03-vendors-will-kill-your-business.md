I really think everyone needs to consider this at all times.

Whether it be for security, operational, reputational and many other reasons: the vendors you choose really can kill your business.

The statement may seem insane and hyperbolic but it is 100% true.

Minimize your vendor count.  Reduce your attack surface.  Don't install every marketing SDK in your app or website.

Vendors will kill your business™
