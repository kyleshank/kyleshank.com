A new version (1.6) of Hypervals is now out for Mac, iOS and Apple watch.

I spent a ton of time testing the crap out of timers and internet blocking synchronizing across all devices.  If you setup Hypervals on iOS and Mac you can start a Superval interval that blocks the internet on both devices for the specified duration.

There are also several significant improvements to the internet blocking. Additionally, like Anatype, support for Apple Music and Spotify during internet blocking has been added. Music is important for focus!

If you need a full primer on Hypervals check out the [release post](/hypervals).

Try it out!
