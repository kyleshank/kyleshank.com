I [started making t-shirts](https://ordinarynouns.com) for some reason and can't stop.  The idea of a shirt that simply says the ordinary nouns with a design evoking what the the intent is instead really got me.  It started with just having the words Major City on a shirt being hilarious to me.  My first design was the Major City NYC shirt where it all came together and made sense (at least to me).

[![NYC](/assets/images/nyc.jpg "Major City NYC")](https://ordinarynouns.com/products/major-city-nyc)

Now the Major City idea is a theme collection of 4 shirts. You get the idea.

[![Vegas](/assets/images/vegas.jpg "Major City Vegas")](https://ordinarynouns.com/products/major-city-vegas)

[![Hills](/assets/images/hills.jpg "Major City Hills")](https://ordinarynouns.com/products/major-city-hills)

[![Hollywood](/assets/images/hollywood.jpg "Major City Hollywood")](https://ordinarynouns.com/products/major-city-hollywood)

One of my favorite branches of this idea is the orange Television shirt in the Nickelodeon font.

[![Television](/assets/images/television.jpg "Television")](https://ordinarynouns.com/products/television)

No AI was used in making these shirts. I hand assembled all of the vectors and even did some custom font letter design in order to match the theme.

I'm calling the brand [Ordinary Nouns](https://ordinarynouns.com) and you can now buy this shit at [ordinarynouns.com](https://ordinarynouns.com).
